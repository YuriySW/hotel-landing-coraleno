01Who we are

Coraleno is a boutique hotel at 77 Flinders Lane, Melbourne VIC 3000, with a kitchen, a bar, a spa-style bathing area and a small, separate gaming room that operates in person only. In this policy, "we", "us" and "Coraleno" mean the business operating from that address.

This policy covers information we collect through this website, by phone and email, and at the venue itself. It does not cover other organisations whose sites you reach by following a link from ours.

02What we collect

We try to collect only what we need. Depending on how you deal with us, that may include:

  • Contact details such as your name, email address and phone number when you use our contact form, call us or write to us.
  • Stay details such as dates, room preferences, accessibility needs and the names of other guests, if you make or change a booking with us.
  • Identity details shown at the door, such as your name, date of birth and photo, taken from your photo ID.
  • Images of you captured by CCTV cameras at the venue.
  • Dietary needs and allergies, which you choose to tell the kitchen.
  • Technical data held in your own browser, such as the age confirmation and cookie choice described in our cookie notice.

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect payment card numbers through this website, and you should not send them to us by email.

03How we use it

We use personal information to answer your questions, manage your stay, serve you in the kitchen and bar, keep the venue safe and meet our legal obligations. Specifically we may use it to:

  • Reply to messages and confirm or change bookings.
  • Prepare your room and meet any accessibility or dietary requests.
  • Confirm that guests are 18 or over before they stay or enter the gaming room.
  • Investigate incidents, complaints and safety concerns.
  • Keep records that we are required to hold by law.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not run advertising profiles on visitors to this site.

04ID and age checks

Because the venue is adults-only, staff check photo ID at arrival and at the foot of the gaming-room stairs. In most cases the check is visual: a staff member looks at your ID and lets you in. We do not keep a copy of your ID for ordinary entry.

We may record limited details, such as your name and the date of the check, where this is necessary to manage a venue exclusion, an incident or a legal requirement. If you ask to be excluded from the gaming room, we will keep a record so that we can honour your request on future visits.

The age notice on this website is a simple prompt and is not a form of identity verification. It stores a simple flag in your browser so you are not asked on each page. The flag contains no name, date of birth or other detail, and we do not use it to identify you.

05CCTV

We operate CCTV in public areas of the venue, including the entrance, lobby, corridors, the kitchen and bar, and the gaming room. Cameras are not placed in guest rooms, bathrooms or changing areas. Signs at the entrance tell you that cameras are in use.

Footage is used for safety, security and the protection of guests and staff. It may be viewed by authorised staff and, where required, provided to police or a regulator. Footage is held for a limited period and then overwritten, unless it is needed for an incident or a legal matter.

06Who we share with

We share personal information only where it is needed to run the venue or where the law requires it. This may include:

  • Service providers that host our website and email, or supply software we use to manage bookings.
  • Police, courts or regulators, when we are legally required to disclose information or need to report a safety issue.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants, lawyers or insurers, who are bound by confidentiality.

Some of our providers may store data outside Australia. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure the information is handled in a way consistent with Australian privacy law.

07How long we keep it

We keep contact enquiries for as long as is needed to answer them and for a reasonable period afterwards in case of follow-up questions. Booking records are kept for the period required for tax and accounting purposes. CCTV footage is overwritten on a rolling basis. When we no longer need information, we delete it or remove details that identify you.

08Security

We use reasonable physical and technical safeguards to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. These include restricted access to records, password-protected systems and training for staff who handle personal details. No system is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information, such as card numbers or ID scans, by email.

09Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it if it is wrong, out of date or incomplete. You can also ask us to delete information we no longer need. To make a request, email hello@coraleno.com or write to us at 77 Flinders Lane, Melbourne VIC 3000. We may ask you to verify who you are before we release anything.

We will respond within a reasonable time. In some cases the law lets us refuse access, for example where it would affect another person's privacy. If we refuse, we will tell you why in writing.

10Complaints and changes

If you think we have mishandled your information, write to us first at hello@coraleno.com and we will investigate. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
