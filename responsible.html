Age

Nobody under 18

Guests must be 18 or over to stay at Coraleno, to enter the gaming room, or to be served alcohol. We check photo ID every time, at the foot of the stairs.

Accepted ID

  • Australian or foreign passport.
  • Australian driver licence.
  • Victorian proof-of-age card.

If you do not have valid ID, you will not be admitted. We do not make exceptions for hotel guests, regulars or staff friends.

Your tools

Make a plan before you play

None of these cost anything. Ask any member of staff and they will help.

Set a limit

Decide how much you can afford to lose and how long you want to stay, before you go down. Write it on your phone. When you reach either, stop.

Take a break

Clocks are visible on the floor. Staff will offer water, a walk to the lane, or a quiet seat upstairs if you have been playing for a long time.

Ask for exclusion

You can ask the duty manager to exclude you from the gaming room for a period you choose, or permanently. We will take you through the process in private.

Signs to watch

When it stops being fun

Gambling harm can build slowly. These are common signs. If any of them sound familiar, speak to someone you trust or call a support line.

  • Spending more money or time than you planned.
  • Chasing losses, or borrowing to play.
  • Hiding how much you gamble from family or friends.
  • Feeling anxious, irritable or low when you are not playing.
  • Missing work, rent or bills because of gambling.

Free support, any hour

For family and friends

On the floor
