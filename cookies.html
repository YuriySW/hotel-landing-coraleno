01What these are

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to keep. Related technologies, such as local storage, work in a similar way: they let a site remember something between pages or visits. We use the term "storage" in this notice to cover both.

Storage is not harmful in itself, and most of what we use is the kind that helps a site do something you asked for. The rest of this notice lists exactly what that is.

02What we store

Coraleno sets only the following items on this site:

  • crl_age_ok (local storage, kept until you clear it). Records that you chose "Yes, I'm 18+" so the age prompt does not appear on every page.
  • crl_age (cookie, one year). A matching flag set at the same moment, so the choice survives if local storage is cleared separately. It holds the value 1 and nothing else.
  • crl_cookie_ok (local storage, kept until you clear it). Records that you dismissed the cookie banner so we do not show it again.

These items are essential to how the site behaves, and none of them contains your name, your date of birth or any other detail that identifies you. The age prompt is a simple notice, not a check of identity. Real ID checks happen in person at the venue.

If you choose "No, I'm under 18", we send you to a page that explains why you cannot continue. We do not store any record of that choice.

03Third-party loads

Some parts of the site are served by other providers. When your browser fetches them, the provider can see technical information such as your IP address and browser type. We do not control how they handle it, so you may want to read their own policies.

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces Syne and Manrope.
  • jsDelivr supplies the Tabler icon set used for small symbols across the site.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies the embedded map in the footer, which shows where 77 Flinders Lane is. The map may set its own storage once it loads.

If you would rather not load these, you can use a browser setting or extension to block them. The site will still work, although it will look plainer and the map will be empty. Our footer also gives the address in text and a link to open the map in a new tab.

04What we do not use

To be plain about it, this site does not use:

  • Advertising or retargeting cookies of any kind.
  • Analytics or heat-map tools that follow what you do on the page.
  • Social media pixels or embedded feeds.
  • Any script that places a bet, opens an account or lets you gamble. Gaming happens only in person at the venue.

If that changes, we will update this notice before the change goes live and ask for your choice where the law requires us to.

05How long things last

Each item has a different lifespan, and you are always in charge of it:

  • The age flag in local storage stays until you clear your browser data. We do not set an expiry on it, because it holds no personal information and does nothing else.
  • The age cookie lasts for twelve months from the day you confirm. After that your browser discards it, and the prompt will return if the local flag is also gone.
  • The cookie-banner flag stays until you clear it. If you clear it, the banner will simply come back on your next visit.

We do not link any of these items to a name, an email address or a booking. If you write to us through the contact form, the details you type are handled under our privacy policy and are not tied to the storage described here.

06Questions people ask

Do you track me across other sites? No. We do not run advertising networks, so nothing here follows you from page to page or site to site.

Why does the age prompt keep coming back? Your browser is probably set to clear site data when you close it, or you are using a private window. The prompt is only as persistent as the storage your browser allows.

Does confirming my age give you my age? No. We store a yes-flag only. We never see a date of birth, and the prompt does not verify anything. Staff check your ID in person.

Can I use the site without any storage? You can read every page, but you will be asked about your age on each new page, because we have nowhere to remember the answer. The contact form still works.

Is the map safe to load? It is a standard embedded OpenStreetMap view. If you would rather not load it, the address is written out in full next to the map and a link opens the map in a separate tab instead.

What about children? This site is meant for adults and the age prompt is there to say so. If a young person lands here, choosing "No, I'm under 18" takes them to a page that stops them going further and points to free gambling support. We do not collect anything from that visit, and no storage is written when that button is used.

07Controlling storage

You can view, block or delete storage from your browser's settings. Look for options named cookies, site data or local storage. Clearing them removes your age flag and cookie choice, so the prompt and banner will appear again on your next visit.

Private or incognito windows discard storage when you close them. If you use one, expect to confirm your age each time you open the site. Blocking all storage will have the same effect, because the site cannot remember your answer.
