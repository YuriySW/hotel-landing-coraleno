The Laneway

Melbourne is a city of lanes, and ours has been a working street for over a century. Step out of the roller door and you are in the middle of it: coffee, art, trams and river within a short walk.

Getting here

Arrive any way you like

There is no car park under the hotel, and that is on purpose. The city around us is built for walking.

By train

Flinders Street Station is the nearest, roughly a six-minute walk. Southern Cross Station is a longer walk west and handles the airport coach. Both are in the city loop, so most lines will get you here.

By tram

Trams run along Swanston Street and Elizabeth Street, each a short walk from our door. Check the Public Transport Victoria journey planner for the latest routes and tram stops on the day you travel.

By car or taxi

Ask your driver for 77 Flinders Lane. The laneway entrance is on the north side of the lane. Taxis and rideshare can stop briefly at the kerb to unload.

By bike

There are lockable racks inside the roller door. Ask the desk for a loan bike if you want to roam the river paths. Helmets are available, and in Victoria they are required by law when riding.

Our local list

What the desk recommends

The front desk keeps a shortlist on a clipboard by the key rack. Ask for the clipboard and tell us what you are in the mood for.

  • Morning: walk to Degraves Street for a second coffee, then follow the lane to the river.
  • Afternoon: Hosier Lane, where the wall art changes constantly. Go early to avoid the crowds.
  • Evening: walk along the Yarra, then come back up to the long bar for a late plate.
  • After dark: the lane is lit but quiet. Keep to the main route home and ask us for a taxi when you need one.
